The builder below is <datum-form-builder layout="contained">: its bar, panels and canvas all sit inside the 720px box the page gave it, and the page keeps its own header and the content below. Drag a component from the palette onto the canvas and the definition on the right updates. persist-panels="demo-builder" makes it remember which strip panel is open, the column widths and the inspector's fold between visits; narrow the window under 900px and the side column and the inspector open as drawers over the canvas instead.
