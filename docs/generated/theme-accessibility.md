# Form accessibility

How the Datum form, drawn by the Datum binding, was checked with axe-core against the WCAG 2.2 rules it can test, in two ways, and tracked here as-is. These are automated checks only: no manual audit has been done, and axe finds only part of what a person would. This document records what the audits found, it does not decide what is fixed. A design-system binding is audited by its own package; this document covers the form the engine ships. Generated by `npm run a11y:report` in `packages/datum` from the committed baselines under `a11y/baseline/`; a test holds it to those baselines.

| Layer | What it sees | What it cannot see | Baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Markup** — axe-core in jsdom | Structure of the rendered form: names, roles, states, relationships, language, list and table semantics, target size markup | Colour, layout, anything a stylesheet decides (jsdom lays nothing out) | `markup.json` |
| **Browser** — axe-core in headless Chrome | The rendered form as painted: everything above plus colour contrast as drawn, under each colour scheme | Hover and focus states (axe measures the static state) | `browser.json` |

States audited: **interactive** (interactive, after an invalid submit), **readonly** (readonly review), **fr-CA** (french (fr-ca), after an invalid submit). Fixture: `packages/datum/a11y/form.json`, one of every standard component.

Versions: axe-core 4.14.1, Chrome/158.0.8127.18.
